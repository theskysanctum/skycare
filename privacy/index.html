Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Short version: your things stay on your phone. We do not collect your data, and we have no way to see it.

The app

  • No accounts, ads, or analytics. The app does not ask who you are and does not report back to us.
  • Everything you add is saved on your phone only. That includes your name, skill answers, plans, moods, diary, food log, profile, notes, reminders, and any journal pages. It leaves your phone only when you tap Export, Share, or save a PDF, and then it goes where you send it.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Apps tab (Android). To show the list of apps you can choose from, the app reads the names and icons of the apps installed on your phone. That list is used on your phone only. The app saves just which ones you checked, and nothing about your apps is sent anywhere.
  • Wallpaper. A wallpaper picture you choose is copied into the app's own storage on your device. It is not included in Export and is never uploaded.
  • Your path. If you pick a religion or spiritual path, that choice is stored on your phone like everything else. It is only used to show the matching journal page.

When the app goes online

The app connects to the internet only to fetch public reference information. It does not send your diary, moods, name, or anything else you wrote. These sites can see your phone's network address, like any website you visit.

  • Moon dates from the U.S. Naval Observatory (aa.usno.navy.mil), when you open the Moon page.
  • Bible verses from bible-api.com, only on the Bible Study page. The passage you type in the search box is sent so it can be found.
  • An ayah of the day from alquran.cloud, only on the Quran and Dua page.
  • The weekly Torah portion from sefaria.org, only on the Torah Study page.

Everything fetched is saved on your phone so it works offline afterward. If you never open these pages, the app never connects.

The web app

The web app at /app/ works like the Android app. Everything you add is saved in your browser's own storage on your device. We never receive it. Your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files so it can work offline. It makes the same few requests listed above, only on the pages that need them. If you clear your browser's site data, your saved things are cleared too, so use Export now and then.

The website

  • This site has no cookies, no analytics, and no ads. Its fonts are served from this site.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The checklist page keeps your answers in your browser's own storage on your device, and nothing is sent to us. It loads its styles from cdn.tailwindcss.com and its font from Google Fonts, so those services can see your network address when the page opens.

Moving or deleting your data

To move to a new phone, use "Export all my data" and "Restore from a file" on the More tab. To delete everything, use "Erase my data" on the More tab or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to delete.
