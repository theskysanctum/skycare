Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Short version: your things stay on your device. We do not collect your data, and we have no way to see it. If you choose to turn on Dropbox sync, a locked copy also goes to your own Dropbox, and only you can open it.

The app

  • No accounts, ads, or analytics. The app does not ask who you are and does not report back to us.
  • Everything you add is saved on your phone. That includes your name, skill answers, plans, moods, diary, food log, profile, notes, reminders, and any journal pages. It leaves your phone only when you tap Export, Share, or save a PDF, and then it goes where you send it, or when you turn on Dropbox sync (below).
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Locked files. When you save your inventory, backup, or profile, you can lock the file with a password. Locking happens on your device, using AES-256-GCM, with the key made from your password by PBKDF2 (600,000 rounds). The password is never saved or sent anywhere, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered by anyone. A file saved without a password is plain text, and anyone who has the file can read it.
  • Apps tab (Android). To show the list of apps you can choose from, the app reads the names and icons of the apps installed on your phone. That list is used on your phone only. The app saves just which ones you checked, and nothing about your apps is sent anywhere.
  • Wallpaper. A wallpaper picture you choose is copied into the app's own storage on your device. It is not included in Export and is never uploaded.
  • Your path. If you pick a religion or spiritual path, that choice is stored on your phone like everything else. It is only used to show the matching journal page.

Dropbox sync (optional)

Dropbox sync is off until you turn it on in More. It is the one time your data leaves your device on its own, and it goes only to your own Dropbox account.

  • What is sent. One file, named skycare.sync, holding the same things an Export holds: your name, plans, moods, diary, food log, notes, reminders, and settings. It is not sent to us. It goes from your device straight to Dropbox.
  • It is locked first. Before it leaves your device, the file is locked with a sync password you choose, using AES-256-GCM with a key made from your password by PBKDF2 (600,000 rounds). Dropbox and we see only scrambled data. The sync password is kept on your device so syncing can happen by itself, and it is never sent anywhere. If you forget it, no one can recover it, and the Dropbox copy cannot be opened.
  • What SkyCare can see in Dropbox. It asks for access to its own app folder only. It cannot see your other files. It also receives a sign-in token that is kept on your device. You can remove SkyCare's access at any time in your Dropbox account's connected apps settings.
  • When it connects. When you open the app, shortly after you change something, and when you tap Sync now, only while Dropbox sync is on. Dropbox can see your network address and handles your file under Dropbox's privacy policy.
  • Turning it off. Tap Disconnect in More. SkyCare forgets the token and the sync password on that device. Your data stays on the device, and the copy stays in your Dropbox until you delete it there.

When the app goes online

The app connects to the internet only to fetch public reference information. Apart from Dropbox sync, if you turn it on, it does not send your diary, moods, name, or anything else you wrote. These sites can see your phone's network address, like any website you visit.

  • Moon dates from the U.S. Naval Observatory (aa.usno.navy.mil), when you open the Moon page.
  • Bible verses from bible-api.com, only on the Bible Study page. The passage you type in the search box is sent so it can be found.
  • An ayah of the day from alquran.cloud, only on the Quran and Dua page.
  • The weekly Torah portion from sefaria.org, only on the Torah Study page.

Everything fetched is saved on your phone so it works offline afterward. If you never open these pages, the app never connects.

The web app

The web app at /app/ works like the Android app. Everything you add is saved in your browser's own storage on your device. We never receive it. If you turn on Dropbox sync in the web app, the same locked file goes to your Dropbox, as described above. Your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files so it can work offline. It makes the same few requests listed above, only on the pages that need them. If you clear your browser's site data, your saved things are cleared too, so use Export now and then.

The website

  • This site has no cookies, no analytics, and no ads. Its fonts are served from this site.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The inventory page keeps your answers in your browser's own storage on your device, and nothing is sent to us. It loads its styles from cdn.tailwindcss.com and its font from Google Fonts, so those services can see your network address when the page opens.

Moving or deleting your data

To move to a new phone, use "Export all my data" and "Restore from a file" on the More tab. You can also use Dropbox sync to share your data between devices. To delete everything, use "Erase my data" on the More tab or uninstall the app, and delete skycare.sync from your Dropbox if you used sync. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to delete.
